Walk and speed

Stride and foot lift are percentages of each joint's calibrated range, so the walk never asks for more than you measured. Speed scales Squat loop, Sweep and Walk. The servo speed limit caps how fast any servo may move, in the picture and on the leg.

Joints

Walks you through each servo: centre, direction and both stops.

Each leg is calibrated on its own tab. Each slider moves one joint. The shaded part is the range its servo can reach. If the real joint moves the opposite way to the picture, open Calibrate and flip its direction.

Setup steps
  1. Flash firmware/bridge/bridge.ino to the Nano with the PCA9685 on A4 and A5. Plug the left leg servos into channels 0 to 4 and the right leg into 5 to 9 (hip yaw, hip pitch, knee, ankle pitch, ankle roll).
  2. Connect, then choose Horn mode. Every servo holds 90°. Fit the horns with each joint straight.
  3. Calibrate each joint: set the centre, then the two mechanical stops.
  4. Choose Run and try Squat and Sweep. Compare the real leg with the picture and flip any joint that disagrees.
